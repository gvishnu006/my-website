About Nova Studio

Nova Studio is a small team obsessed with performance. We believe a website should load in a blink, work on any device, and be easy to maintain.

Everything here is hand-written: semantic HTML, modern CSS, and a few lines of vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no lock-in.

Our principles

Simple — fewer moving parts mean fewer things that break.

Fast — every kilobyte has to earn its place.

Inclusive — built for keyboards, screen readers and slow networks.
